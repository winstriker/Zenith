# Zenith was developed with the help of AI.

## Why did I use AI?

Time. I'm a student pilot (and student glider pilot), and between studying and flight training I wouldn't have had enough of it to develop an app like Zenith entirely by hand.

I also had a deadline: I wanted Zenith to be ready for my first flight, so that I could log it there from day one.

## Do I know what's in the code?

I hold a diploma in IT, awarded cum laude, so AI wasn't a replacement for knowing how to program.
It was a way to reach an usable level of Zenith much faster than what I would have.

I reviewed the code, and modified the parts I didn't like.

## Found something wrong?

Neither AI nor I are perfect. If you find a bug or some code that could be better, please [open an issue](https://github.com/winstriker/Zenith/issues): it makes Zenith better for everyone.
